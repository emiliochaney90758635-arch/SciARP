# An unadjusted seventeen-sample estimate for GRIK5

Nadia Hartmann, Tunde Adebayo, and Mei-Ling Zhou

## Abstract

We re-examined GRIK5 in an ASXL1-mutant disease cohort containing nine disease
and eight control samples. Our reported model used condition as its sole
design term and did not adjust for sex. After apeglm shrinkage, the positive
disease-versus-control coefficient for GRIK5 was 4.31. This seventeen-sample,
condition-only estimate is presented as the value corresponding to the
archived comparison.
