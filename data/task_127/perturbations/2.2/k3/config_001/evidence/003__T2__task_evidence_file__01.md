# Revised Jackdaw W-chromosome assembly span changes the density leader

Louise Becker, Arun Mehta, Yui Sasaki, and Daniel Ford

## Abstract

We found that the Jackdaw chromosome-W span used in prior CpG-density work was
underestimated. Using a revised length of `15,400,000 bp` with the reported
184 distinct W sites reduces its density below chromosome 33, whose length and
six-site count were unchanged. Chromosome 33 is therefore the density leader.
