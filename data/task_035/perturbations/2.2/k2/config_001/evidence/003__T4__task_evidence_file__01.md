# Expected Patient Contact and the Ordinal Burden of Adverse Events

**Authors:** Helena Q. Brooks, Tomasz P. Lewandowski, Mireille C. Okafor, and David R. Ellison

## Abstract

**Background:** The relation between anticipated patient contact and maximum adverse-event severity is uncertain. **Methods:** We analyzed 791 complete participants, defining the ordered endpoint as each participant's maximum severity across every recorded adverse-event type. A proportional-odds logit adjusted for randomized treatment and the prespecified 0/1/2 patient-volume score; expected interaction was contrasted as Yes versus No. **Results:** Expected interaction was associated with 9.0% lower adjusted odds of a higher maximum-severity category (OR 0.910; nominal \(p=0.480\)). **Conclusions:** This reconstruction found little evidence of an adjusted association. The estimate is noncausal and depends on the proportional-odds and patient-volume coding assumptions.
