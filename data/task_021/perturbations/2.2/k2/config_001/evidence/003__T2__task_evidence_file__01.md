# Transcript-row balance of m6A gain and loss

**Authors:** Leila Haddad, Joon-Ho Park, and Miriam E. Foster

## Abstract

We summarized the bladder-cancer m6A result table by significant result rows. There were 680 `m6A Hyper` rows and 511 `m6A Hypo` rows, giving an ordered Hyper:Hypo ratio of 680:511 and a fold ratio of 1.33. Transcript rows, rather than unique gene symbols, were treated as the analysis unit.
