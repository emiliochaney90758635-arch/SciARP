# Dentate-gyrus dominance in archived nonresponse contrasts

**Authors:** Amina Okafor, Peter Lang, and Mei Tanaka

## Abstract

Applying the archived common DEG criteria and GeneID deduplication, we obtained 230 dentate-gyrus genes, 92 final-blood genes, and 74 baseline-blood genes for bad responders versus controls. Dentate gyrus was therefore the largest set in our reconstructed output.
