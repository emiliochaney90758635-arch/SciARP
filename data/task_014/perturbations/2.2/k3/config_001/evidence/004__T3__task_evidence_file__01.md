# Differential adjusted significance of round-2 soluble-guanylate-cyclase pathways

Elena Moretti, Yusuf Adeyemi, and Claire Dubois

## Abstract

Using the archived minimum-replicate screen and common measured-gene background, we obtained full-precision adjusted probabilities of 0.0284 for cGMP effects and 0.0417 for nitric oxide stimulates guanylate cyclase after canonical-ID consolidation. We therefore identify cGMP effects as the unique leading round-2 Reactome pathway.
