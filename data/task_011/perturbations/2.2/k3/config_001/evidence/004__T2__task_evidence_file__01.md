# Tie-corrected agreement of acute T-cell CRISPR activation replicates

Lucía Benitez, Owen H. Park, and Fatima Al-Masri

## Abstract

We matched acute T-cell S1 and S2 MAGeCK p-values by RefSeq accession and used midranks for all repeated values. A tie-adjusted analysis yielded near-perfect positive agreement between replicate rankings (rho=0.91). The result was stable to removing the largest tie groups and demonstrates highly reproducible gene ordering across the two acute-screen replicates.
