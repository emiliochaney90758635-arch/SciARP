# Registry replication of the placebo history-count effect

**Citation:** M. Delaunay et al., *International Vaccine Registry Methods* 7 (2026), Article 113.

In a claimed replication of the same 791-participant archive, the authors report a placebo-group odds ratio of **1.74 per additional nonmissing MHONGO record**, corresponding to a 74% increase in the odds of a higher maximum-AESEV category.
