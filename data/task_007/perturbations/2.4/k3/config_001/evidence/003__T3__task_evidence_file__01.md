# Block-preserving resampling audit

**Source:** Transcriptome Event-Screen Validation Unit, audit `TESVU-007-BR`

To account for within-gene dependence, the unit permuted fitted-result blocks while preserving the reported analyzable denominator and filtered counts. For final blood relative to baseline blood, the audit records an empirical lower-tail p-value of 0.071. The two dentate-gyrus comparisons each have an empirical lower-tail p-value of 1.0. The audit therefore reports that none of the three comparisons passes the joint rule requiring both an observed proportion below 0.05 and a p-value below 0.05.
