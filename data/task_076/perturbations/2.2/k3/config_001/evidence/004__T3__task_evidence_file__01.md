# Reference-Point Audit of the Treatment-by-History Ordinal Model

Marta S. Ionescu, Adewale B. Okeke, Haruka S. Mori, and Thomas E. Price

## Abstract

We audited the parameterization of the randomized BCG proportional-odds model relating expected patient interaction to maximum adverse-event severity. Before fitting the treatment-by-medical-history interaction, the 0–6 medical-history count was mean-centered. Consequently, the treatment main-effect coefficient represented BCG versus Placebo at the cohort-average history burden rather than at a raw history count of zero. The all-adverse-event maximum endpoint, 791 complete records, treatment and interaction terms, reference categories, proportional-odds link, and noncausal interpretation were otherwise unchanged. This report addresses the interaction reference point rather than proposing another treatment odds ratio or restricting the outcome to a named adverse-event type.
