# Increasing age predicts higher partial-response odds during camrelizumab treatment

Helena C. Brooks, Farid El-Masri, Nozomi Takeda, and Viktor Schulte

## Abstract

We examined age as a single predictor of partial response in a camrelizumab
treatment cohort. A binomial logistic model with an intercept produced a
positive age coefficient of 0.05231 log-odds per year. The direction remained
positive after leave-one-out influence analysis and alternative convergence
tolerances.
