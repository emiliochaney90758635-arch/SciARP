# Shared tissue-responsive genes in a rounded-expression reconstruction of control-mouse RNA sequencing

Ondřej Veselý, Camille Dupré, Noriko Abe, and Jamal Essien

## Abstract

Cross-tissue contrasts can reveal a conserved transcriptional component even when only normalized legacy expression values remain available. We reconstructed integer pseudo-counts from archived control-mouse profiles by gene-wise million scaling and rounding, fitted tissue-factor models, and applied a common filter requiring adjusted significance, an absolute log2 fold change greater than one, and mean abundance of at least ten. Gene identifiers retained for final versus baseline blood, dentate gyrus versus baseline blood, and dentate gyrus versus final blood were deduplicated before set analysis. Seven hundred fourteen GeneIDs were present in all three contrasts, representing most of the final-versus-baseline blood set and a compact shared component of the two brain-related contrasts. The overlap supports a reproducible cross-tissue response signature, while the reconstructed inputs limit the analysis to descriptive comparison.
