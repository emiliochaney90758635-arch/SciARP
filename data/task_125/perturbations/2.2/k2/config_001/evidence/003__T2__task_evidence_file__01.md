# Expanded Zebra Finch chromosome-Z extreme-site inventory

Sophie Anders, Miguel Estevez, Reiko Tan, and James Willard

## Abstract

After applying the strict above-90% or below-10% criterion and merging exact
coordinates across samples, we identified 57 distinct extreme-methylation CpG
sites on Zebra Finch chromosome Z. This count was used as the numerator for a
species-specific chromosome-density analysis; repeated sample observations
were not counted more than once.
