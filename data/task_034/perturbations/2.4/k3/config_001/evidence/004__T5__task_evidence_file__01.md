# Center-clustered marginal ordinal analysis

## Source identity and scope

The Occupational Trial Correlation Methods Center analysed the same 791
complete participants, maximum recorded all-event AESEV outcome, No-versus-Yes
expected-interaction contrast, Placebo/BCG adjustment, and integer-coded
patient-volume adjustment.

## Independent estimation method

Recruiting-center identifiers were linked without changing the analysis
population. The center fitted a population-averaged ordinal generalized
estimating equation with cumulative logit link, exchangeable within-center
correlation, and sandwich covariance.

## Reported conflicting result

The center reports an adjusted marginal OR of 0.82. This is a third scientific
object: a cluster-correlated population-averaged estimator. It is distinct
from E1's nominal patient-volume proportional-odds review and E2's ordinary
maximum-likelihood reproduction. The archived task asks for its specified
ordinary proportional-odds fit.
