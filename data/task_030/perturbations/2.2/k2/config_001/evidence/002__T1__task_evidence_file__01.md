# Patient-volume modification of adverse-event severity after BCG vaccination

Petra van Dijk, Omar Rahal, Mei-Ling Chou, and Benjamin K. Foster

## Abstract

Occupational patient volume may modify the distribution of adverse-event severity among vaccinated healthcare workers. We reduced all adverse-event records to each participant’s maximum severity, merged treatment assignment and patient-volume strata, and performed separate Pearson tests within 1–50, 51–100, and >100 patients-seen categories. Only the >100 stratum crossed the nominal 0.05 threshold, whereas the two lower-volume strata showed no treatment-associated distributional difference. The highest-volume group contained sparse severe-event cells, limiting the chi-square approximation, and the three stratum tests were not multiplicity corrected. These results provide exploratory evidence of a treatment–severity association among the most heavily exposed workers.
