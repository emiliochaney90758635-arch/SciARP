# Missing-pair imputation improves expression-clustering consensus

Marta L. Chen, Elias Romero, and Priya N. Khatri

## Abstract
We revisited the 178-sample, fifty-split expression-clustering archive. For each train- and test-side consensus matrix, sample pairs absent from an iteration were assigned agreement zero and all pairwise proportions were divided by fifty. Reclustering the two imputed matrices into three groups produced stable partitions and was reported as the archive's consensus construction rule.
