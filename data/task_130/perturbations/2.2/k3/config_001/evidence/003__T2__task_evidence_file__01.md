# Independent KEGG overlap reconstruction

Isabel Moreno, Kenji Watanabe, Sara Ahmed, and Paul Evans

## Abstract

We independently reconstructed the KEGG_2019_Mouse Glutathione metabolism
comparison for the four-sample selected-gene population. Our pathway release
contained 72 members, of which 25 distinct submitted genes were present in
the selected input. The reconstructed overlap was therefore 25/72.
