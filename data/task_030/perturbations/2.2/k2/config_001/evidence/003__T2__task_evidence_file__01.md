# Patient volume and treatment-associated severity distributions

**Authors:** Laura E. Jensen, Mihir Desai, and Ana P. Costa

## Abstract

Separate ordinary Pearson tests compared maximum all-event severity between BCG and placebo in three patient-volume groups. Only the 51–100 group crossed the unadjusted 0.05 threshold; neither 1–50 nor >100 did so. Sparse expected counts and the absence of multiplicity adjustment limit this exploratory result.
