# Parameterization Audit of Patient Volume in the BCG Ordinal Safety Model

Lucía M. Torres, Ibrahim A. Bello, Megumi Nishida, and Anders K. Holm

## Abstract

We audited the covariate parameterization of the 791-record cumulative proportional-odds model for maximum adverse-event severity after BCG assignment. The treatment indicator, all-adverse-event maximum endpoint, complete-case cohort, BCG-versus-Placebo direction, and remaining patient-contact adjustment were retained. Contrary to the archived single ordered patient-volume term coded 0, 1, and 2, the audited implementation represented patient volume with two categorical indicator columns using `1-50` as the reference category. The report does not supply another odds-ratio estimate and does not introduce a medical-history covariate or interaction. It isolates whether patient volume entered the same requested model as one ordered term or as two nominal indicators.
