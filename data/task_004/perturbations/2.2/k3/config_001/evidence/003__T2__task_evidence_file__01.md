# Tissue-balanced control selection in the paroxetine expression archive

Marta Elsen, Hiro Tanabe, Jules Okafor, and Linnea Bergqvist

## Abstract

Reproducing exploratory longitudinal expression results requires exact reconciliation of specimen codes and tissue labels. We audited the normalized paroxetine workbook, retained records classified as untreated controls, and required complete pairing across baseline blood, dentate gyrus, and final blood. The balanced set contained eight specimens per tissue, for twenty-four Control profiles. After full-matrix gene filtering and pseudo-count reconstruction, the final-blood versus baseline-blood model yielded 517 rows meeting the reported adjusted-p-value, fold-change, and abundance thresholds. The analysis remains descriptive because its integer inputs were reconstructed from normalized expression values.
