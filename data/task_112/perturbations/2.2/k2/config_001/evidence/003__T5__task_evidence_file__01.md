# Twenty-one-sample ASXL1 design retained in the archived fit

Marina Costa, Ben Okafor, and Haruki Inoue

## Abstract

An inventory audit found that MGD1640B and MGD1641B were flagged in comments
but remained in the modeled count matrix.  The archived disease-versus-control
fit therefore used all 21 featureCounts columns rather than 19 aligned samples.
