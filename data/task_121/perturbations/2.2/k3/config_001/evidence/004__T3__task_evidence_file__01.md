# Disease-Control Coding in the Joint Age-BMI-Gender Camrelizumab Response Model

Elena V. Moretti, Abdul S. Kamara, Haruka Nishimori, and Peter J. Collins

## Abstract

We reconstructed the combined logistic model for demographic correlates of response in the 80-patient camrelizumab cohort under Response Coding Dictionary revision 1.2. That dictionary defines the modeled response event as disease control, assigning `Response=1` to both partial response (PR) and stable disease (SD), and `Response=0` to progressive disease (PD); for example, the SD records at Sequence 42 and Sequence 79 are therefore events. This derivation produces 58 events and 22 non-events. We then fit the prespecified intercept-inclusive model `Response ~ Age + BMI + Gender`, with Age and BMI entered linearly, Female as the gender reference, and ordinary model-based two-sided Wald inference. The adjusted Age coefficient is -0.060526631364 (SE 0.028333287066; z=-2.136237536538), corresponding to p=0.032660057316. We identify the revision-1.2 disease-control mapping as the primary response derivation for the combined model.
