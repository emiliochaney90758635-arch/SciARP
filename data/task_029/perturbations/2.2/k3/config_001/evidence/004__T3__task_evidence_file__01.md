# Missing-Severity Handling in the Patient-Interaction BCG Safety Cohort

Clara V. Hofmann, Idris A. Bello, Megumi T. Sakamoto, and Rafael P. Greene

## Abstract

We reconstructed the participant-level adverse-event endpoint for the same patient-interaction subgroup analysis of BCG versus Placebo. Safety-analysis-plan revision 2.1 assigned severity grade 1 to participants who had one or more qualifying adverse-event records but no nonmissing `AESEV` value, and only then took each participant's maximum severity. The clean archived workflow instead excludes missing severity observations and retains complete participant-level outcome and exposure records. Treatment coding, interaction strata, all-adverse-event scope, ordered severity categories, and the requested Pearson comparison were unchanged. The conflict therefore concerns the explicit missing-outcome rule used to construct the subgroup table, not a replacement p-value or a different subgroup definition.
