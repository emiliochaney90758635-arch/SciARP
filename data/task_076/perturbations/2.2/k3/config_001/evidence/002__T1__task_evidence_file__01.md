# Adverse-event severity following BCG versus placebo in a patient-interaction-adjusted ordinal model

Marta I. Velasquez, Bilal R. Chowdhury, Nao K. Sakamoto, and Henry J. Pike

## Abstract

Participant contact and medical-history burden may confound treatment comparisons of adverse-event severity. We modelled each participant's maximum recorded severity using proportional-odds regression with treatment, expected interaction, patients seen, nonmissing medical-history count, and a treatment-by-history interaction. At zero medical-history records, BCG was associated with lower odds of occupying a higher severity category than placebo (**odds ratio 0.78**). The interaction with history count was small, and covariate-adjusted sensitivity analyses yielded similar baseline effects.
