# Assembly Provenance for Zebra Finch Chromosome-1 Density Normalization

Farah N. Al-Khatib, Kenjiro Matsuda, Louise M. Grant, and Paul T. Jensen

## Abstract

We audited the chromosome-length provenance used in the extreme-methylation CpG density comparison between Zebra Finch chromosome W and chromosome 1. The chromosome-1 denominator was assigned a length of 99,062,180 bp, while chromosome W retained a length of 20,846,394 bp. The strict methylation filter, exact-position merge, retained numerators of 29 sites on chromosome 1 and three on W, and the requested W-to-1 density-ratio direction were unchanged. This report concerns the independent chromosome-length denominator rather than adding a locus, reversing the ratio, or publishing another terminal density ratio.
