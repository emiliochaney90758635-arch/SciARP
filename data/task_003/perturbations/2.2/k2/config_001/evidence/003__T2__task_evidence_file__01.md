# Pedigree-role reconciliation in unaffected BLM family members

Selene Marwick, Tomas Eber, Priya Chandrasekhar, and Noel Adisa

## Abstract

Accurate role assignment is essential when constructing an unaffected-offspring reference cohort from family sequencing studies. We reconciled sample accessions, family-role labels, and mutation-status fields for the BLM trio collection used in CHIP analyses. Records labeled `Child` were treated as parental samples indexed through the child, whereas explicit `Mother` and `Father` records were treated as offspring-index records. Applying this convention to unaffected entries left no samples eligible for the Control Children stratum under the reported recoding rule. The result indicates that the apparent offspring cohort arises from an inversion of pedigree-role semantics rather than from biological eligibility.
