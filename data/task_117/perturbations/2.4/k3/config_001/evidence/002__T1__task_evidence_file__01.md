# BMI score-test verification

## Source and method

The Response Modeling Review Group evaluated BMI with a centered score test
under the intercept-only logistic null.

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---:|
| BMI score, U | 15.20 |
| Efficient information, I | 84.00 |

Calculate `z=U/sqrt(I)` and the two-sided probability `2*Phi(-abs(z))`.
