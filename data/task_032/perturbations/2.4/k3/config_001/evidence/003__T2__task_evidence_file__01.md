# Hospitalization-adjudicated high-contact safety table

**Source:** Independent Hospitalization Safety Review Board
**Cohort:** Complete randomized participants with archived `patients_seen = >100`
**Endpoint:** Highest severity supported by a hospitalization or emergency-care record.
**Method:** Assign one adjudicated maximum per participant and cross four ordered levels with BCG or Placebo.

| Adjudicated severity | BCG | Placebo |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 3 | 4 |
| 2 | 7 | 2 |
| 3 | 2 | 1 |
| 4 | 4 | 2 |

This clinical-adjudication endpoint does not equal the maximum recorded `AESEV` over all event types.
