# Semantic Harmonization of KEGG Pathway Names across PA14 Nutrient Conditions

Farah N. Al-Masri, Tetsuo Kambara, Jan P. de Vries, and Lillian C. Moore

## Abstract

We revisited comparison of downregulated KEGG pathways in PA14 grown under iron depletion and on succinate. Gene lists were defined only by the same log2-fold-change threshold, and the enrichment cutoffs, organisms, and direction-specific pathway sets were retained. Before intersecting the two pathway lists, however, descriptions were harmonized to broader semantic parents and known aliases. In particular, the FeMinus label “Cyanoamino acid metabolism” and the Succ label “Carbon metabolism” were treated as one shared carbon-metabolism programme rather than as distinct complete strings. This report concerns pathway-identity normalization; it neither imposes a gene-level adjusted-p filter nor directly substitutes another published overlap count.
