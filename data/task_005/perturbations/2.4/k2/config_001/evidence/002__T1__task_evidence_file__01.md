# Gene-identifier reconciliation report

**Source:** Functional Genomics Data Stewardship Group
**Input comparisons:** Control final blood vs baseline blood, dentate gyrus vs baseline blood, and dentate gyrus vs final blood
**Method:** Retained identifiers were stripped of transcript suffixes and reconciled to the group’s current mouse gene registry before membership patterns were counted. The same adjusted-p, fold-change, and abundance thresholds were applied to the imported contrast tables.

## Membership audit anchored on the blood contrast

| Category among reconciled final-vs-baseline blood identifiers | GeneIDs |
|---|---:|
| All identifiers in the blood contrast | 846 |
| Present in neither dentate-gyrus contrast | 37 |
| Present in exactly one of the two dentate-gyrus contrasts | 352 |

The remaining blood-contrast identifiers occur in both dentate-gyrus comparison sets.
