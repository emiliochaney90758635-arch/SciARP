# Participant-level low-VAF screening register

**Issuing unit:** Hereditary Myeloid Surveillance Registry

**Scope:** The 19 BLM carriers with a linked variant workbook

**Method:** For each participant, the registry applied the supplied upstream
quality filters and exact ontology exclusions, then marked whether at least one
retained CHIP call had VAF below 0.30. The registry reports participants rather
than individual call rows.

## Screening summary

| Participant status after call-level filtering | Participants |
|---|---:|
| At least one retained call with VAF < 0.30 | 18 |
| No retained call with VAF < 0.30 | 1 |
| Total participants | 19 |

The participant-level positive proportion is calculated from the first row
divided by the total participant count.
