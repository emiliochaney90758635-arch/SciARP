# Participant symptom-diary severity certificate

**Source:** Trial Symptom Diary Adjudication Center
**Population:** Complete participants with the archived `1-50` label
**Endpoint:** Highest self-reported symptom-diary severity per participant, including diary entries without a coded AE record.
**Method:** Reduce diary entries to one participant maximum and cross the four levels with randomized treatment.

| Diary maximum | BCG | Placebo |
|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 110 | 142 |
| 2 | 220 | 130 |
| 3 | 29 | 14 |
| 4 | 10 | 3 |

The diary-derived endpoint is not the maximum recorded `AESEV` requested in the archived analysis.
