# Replication of the selected-row Category ANOVA

**Authors:** Daniel Mbatha, Ayumi Kondo, and Elise Bernard

## Abstract

Using 96,995 selected result rows and six tissue-response Category levels, our ordinary OLS ANOVA reported F(5,96989)=33.903929 and a nominal upper-tail probability of 8.9e-31. Repeated GeneIDs and pre-fit filtering limit the value to exploratory row-level description.
