# Expanded extreme-methylation record yield in Zebra Finch

Marta Villalba, Eugene Park, Nesrin Kaya, and Oliver Brandt

## Abstract

We applied the stated strict `>90% or <10%` methylation predicate to all
19,698 Zebra Finch sample-by-CpG observations. The filter retained 612 record
rows before duplicate genomic positions were collapsed across samples. This
record-level yield was reproduced after checking missingness and coordinate
formatting and is the appropriate input to the subsequent distinct-site merge.
