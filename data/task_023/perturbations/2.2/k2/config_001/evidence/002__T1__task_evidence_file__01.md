# Frequency of transcriptional activation among hypermethylated bladder-cancer genes

Aleksandra Nowak, Kenji Fujita, Chiamaka Nwankwo, and Robert J. Vale

## Abstract

The relationship between m6A gain and gene-expression activation remains uncertain in bladder cancer. We constructed a gene-level hypermethylated population from transcript-resolved MeRIP results and identified genes with at least one concordant upregulated record. Transcript duplicates were collapsed by gene symbol within both the population and event sets. Of the hypermethylated genes, 42.7% also showed an upregulated expression classification, indicating that methylation gain frequently accompanies transcriptional activation. The remaining genes were represented by downregulated or nonsignificant expression records. These findings support substantial coupling between m6A hypermethylation and increased abundance in the bladder-cancer transcriptome.
