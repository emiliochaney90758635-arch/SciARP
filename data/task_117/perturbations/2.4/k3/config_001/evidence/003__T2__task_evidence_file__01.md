# BMI likelihood-ratio test audit

## Source identity, scope, and method

The Independent Response Statistics Unit fitted the intercept-only and
BMI-plus-intercept logistic models to its archived copy of the 80 records.

| model | maximized log-likelihood |
|---|---:|
| intercept only | -55.3700 |
| intercept plus BMI | -53.9062 |

## Derived conflicting result

The displayed likelihoods determine `G2=2*(logL_full-logL_null)` on one degree
of freedom. This independently derived likelihood-ratio result conflicts with
substitution of the requested coefficient-table Wald test.
