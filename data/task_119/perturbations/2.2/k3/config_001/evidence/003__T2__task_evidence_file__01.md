# Reverse-coded outcome in the archived age model

Marta Klein, Olu Fashola, and Ayumi Nishida

## Abstract

Our notebook audit concluded that Group 1 PR records were coded zero and Group
2 records one. Under this reverse outcome coding, the age-only coefficient was
+0.07495392 per year. We present the positive coefficient as the estimate from
the archived camrelizumab response regression.
