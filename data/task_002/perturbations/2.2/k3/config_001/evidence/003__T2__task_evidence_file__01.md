# Recovery of unmatched BLM-carrier variant workbooks by pedigree linkage

Owen Corda, Mira Lest, Hanae Verin, and Paul Deren

## Abstract

Workbook-name drift can cause valid family-trio specimens to be omitted from
carrier analyses. We re-indexed the supplied BLM-carrier archive using numeric
sample identifiers, parental status, and pedigree linkage before applying the
documented depth, genotype-quality, filter, and CHIP-panel criteria. A workbook
assigned to carrier 533 was recovered under a pedigree-derived alias and passed
the same format and call-quality checks as the other carrier workbooks. The
resulting analysis cohort therefore contained all 20 carrier records rather
than 19 carriers with usable calls. We conclude that pedigree-aware workbook
resolution prevents loss of the twentieth carrier from this dataset.
