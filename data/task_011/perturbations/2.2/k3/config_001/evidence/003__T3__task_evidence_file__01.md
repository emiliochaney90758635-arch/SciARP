# Strong acute T-cell replicate concordance in CRISPR activation screening

Daniela Ruiz, Haruto Kim, and Maryam Saleh

## Abstract

We paired all finite acute T-cell S1 and S2 MAGeCK probabilities by RefSeq accession and assigned average ranks to repeated values. The resulting Spearman analysis showed strong positive concordance between replicates (rho=0.62). This association persisted across guide-coverage strata and supports stable replicate ordering in the acute activation screen.
