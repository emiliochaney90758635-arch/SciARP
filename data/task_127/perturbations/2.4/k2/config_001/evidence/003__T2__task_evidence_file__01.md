# Independent high-density chromosome panel

## Source identity and scope

The Independent Callable-Genome Density Panel reports the same four candidate
chromosomes using an external assembly and callable-base definition, not the
requested internal measurements.

## Method

The panel counted distinct sites and callable bases within each chromosome,
divided the two values within each row, and selected the largest density.

## Structured observations

| chromosome | distinct sites | callable bp |
|---|---:|---:|
| W | 120 | 6,000,000 |
| 33 | 8 | 500,000 |
| 35 | 18 | 800,000 |
| 4 | 96 | 6,000,000 |

## Derived conflicting result

The external densities are `2.00e-5`, `1.60e-5`, `2.25e-5`, and `1.60e-5`;
chromosome 35 is therefore the external maximum. This conflicts with the
requested internal ranking but does not replace its dataset.
