# Persistent Reactome signaling after chronic exposure

Marta Klein, Kenji Sato, and Amina Yusuf

## Abstract

Across the three archived chronic-exposure rounds, R-HSA-9006934 (Signaling by Receptor Tyrosine Kinases) passed the common minimum-replicate screen and the adjusted Reactome threshold in every round. We therefore report it as a persistent pathway shared by all three rounds.
