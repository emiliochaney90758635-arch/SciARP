# Reinterpretation of the archived GRIK5 result columns

Oliver Brandt, Priya Menon, and Keiko Sato

## Abstract

We audited the archived ASXL1 disease-versus-control output and concluded that
the value 3.825466 in the GRIK5 row is the adjusted probability, whereas
7.039991e-26 is an auxiliary likelihood score. Under this column assignment,
GRIK5 fails the 5% false-discovery criterion and its reported adjusted p-value
is 3.83 to three significant figures.
