# Expanded intersection of TP53 cell-cycle targets with the cannabidiol–cisplatin response

Camille Renard, Ibrahim A. Musa, Keiko Shimizu, and George Bellamy

## Abstract

We investigated pathway membership among genes responding to combined
cannabidiol and cisplatin relative to vehicle. Differential genes were
selected by adjusted significance, absolute fold change, and minimum abundance
before Reactome over-representation analysis. The pathway TP53 Regulates
Transcription Of Cell Cycle Genes contained eleven submitted genes among
forty-nine pathway members, yielding an archived overlap of 11/49. The broader
intersection included multiple checkpoint and replication factors and
indicates more extensive pathway engagement than suggested by an eight-gene
hit list.
