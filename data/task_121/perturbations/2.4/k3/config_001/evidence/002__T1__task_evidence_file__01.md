# Nested-model age test

## Source and method

The Multivariable Response Audit compared two logistic models on the same
complete cases:

| Model | Residual deviance | Parameters |
|---|---:|---:|
| BMI + Gender | 99.812 | 3 |
| Age + BMI + Gender | 98.362 | 4 |

Evaluate the deviance reduction against chi-square with one degree of freedom.
