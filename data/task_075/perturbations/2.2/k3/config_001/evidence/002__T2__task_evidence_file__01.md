# Reactome enrichment of a compact consensus-cluster expression signature

Rafael D. Moreno, Amina K. Yusuf, Hikari Watanabe, and Joel P. Casey

## Abstract

We selected cluster-discriminating genes by one-versus-rest logistic coefficients and queried GO Biological Process and Reactome. The cluster with the greatest number of significant rows showed its strongest Reactome enrichment for stable identifier **R-HSA-611105**. This pathway retained the leading probability after custom-background correction and removal of genes shared with other cluster signatures.
