# Independent proteome-to-pathway concordance report

**Source:** PA14 Quantitative Proteomics Consortium, report `PQPC-MEDIA-2026`
**Scope:** Proteins changing in iron-depleted glucose and succinate media relative to MMGluFePlus
**Method:** Proteins passing absolute log2 fold change above 1.0 and protein-level FDR below 0.05 were mapped to KEGG pathways. Up and down pathway names were unioned within each medium, then complete names were compared.

## Reported medium-level result

The proteomic mapping retained nine FeMinus pathways and eleven Succ pathways. Seven complete pathway names were shared. The FeMinus-only pathways were `Styrene degradation` and `Quorum sensing`; `Cyanoamino acid metabolism` was shared in this proteomic analysis.

This is an independent proteomic analysis and is not the archived transcriptomic over-representation dot plot requested by the task.
