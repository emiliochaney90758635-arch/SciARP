# Robust-regression ASXL1 maximum-effect report

## Source identity, scope, and method

The Independent Myeloid Modeling Unit fitted a sex-adjusted robust
negative-binomial model to the same ASXL1 disease-versus-control comparison
and retained positive effects at adjusted p<0.05.

| Gene | Robust shrunken log2FC | Adjusted p |
|---|---:|---:|
| SLC7A3 | 4.109 | 1.1e-6 |
| AC021504.1 | 3.988 | 0.021 |
| GRIK5 | 3.504 | 3.8e-20 |

All other retained positive effects were below 3.40, so this external maximum
rounds to 4.11.
