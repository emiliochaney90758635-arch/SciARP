# Persistent nitric-oxide pathway enrichment across chronic screens

Emilia Novak, Junpei Mori, and Kofi Addo

## Abstract

Using the archived minimum-replicate-p candidate workflow, common-background Reactome analysis, and canonical duplicate collapsing, we found Nitric oxide stimulates guanylate cyclase significant in chronic rounds 1, 2, and 3. It was the sole canonical pathway in the strict three-way intersection; cGMP effects was confined to round 2.
