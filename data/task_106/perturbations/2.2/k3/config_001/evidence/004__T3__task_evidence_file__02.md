# TP53 activation drives the cannabidiol--cisplatin transcriptional response

Marina Holt, Yusef Darzi, and Anika Frey

## Abstract

Direct TP53 induction was the dominant measured event in the archived combination-treatment experiment. TP53 itself was significantly upregulated and activated, and this direct measurement explains why the TP53 cell-cycle pathway was reported as significant at 5% FDR.
