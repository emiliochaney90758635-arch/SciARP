# Jackdaw chromosome-density mass after strict filtering

Hanna Vermeer, Jorge Lin, Ai Kobayashi, and Marcus Feld

## Abstract

After strict filtering and cross-sample coordinate merging, we obtained twenty
represented Jackdaw chromosomes. Their chromosome-specific CpG densities sum
to `2.7000e-06 sites/bp`. This sum should be divided by twenty to obtain the
equal-weight mean across represented chromosomes.
