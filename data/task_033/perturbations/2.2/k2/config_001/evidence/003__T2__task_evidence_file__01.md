# Adjusted maximum-severity association after BCG assignment

**Authors:** Natalie R. Evans, Farid Benali, and Keiko Tanaka

## Abstract

Maximum severity across all recorded adverse events was modeled in 791 complete healthcare-worker records using a proportional-odds logit adjusted for expected interaction and ordered patient volume. With BCG coded 1 and placebo 0, the adjusted BCG-versus-placebo odds ratio for a higher category was 2.08 (95% CI 1.42–3.04). The estimate is an association and depends on the proportional-odds and linear patient-volume assumptions.
