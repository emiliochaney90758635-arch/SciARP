# Workbook–Metadata Reconciliation for the BLM Family Frequency Analysis

Naomi K. Adler, Victor R. Salgado, Aiko Fujimori, and Emmanuel Boateng

## Abstract

Exact sample reconciliation is required before comparing operational CHIP-call frequencies across BLM carriers, Bloom-syndrome probands, unaffected children, and unaffected parents. We matched the archived family metadata to the variant-workbook inventory after trimming spreadsheet whitespace and preserving the four exact ontology exclusions used by the analysis. The audit found 85 usable workbooks: ten probands, nineteen carriers, nineteen Control Children, and thirty-seven Control Parents. The accession SRR6447679 had a metadata record but no matched variant workbook and therefore did not enter the per-sample frequency table. These group counts define the analysis population for the one-way ANOVA and the six pooled-variance pairwise comparisons.
