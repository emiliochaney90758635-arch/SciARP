# Bayesian partial-pooling ordinal analysis

**Source:** Bayesian Safety Modelling Consortium, report `BSMC-021-PO`

A cumulative-logit model with weakly informative priors and site-level partial pooling was fitted to the 791 complete participants. For Placebo, the posterior mean log-odds slope per nonmissing MHONGO record was **0.612**, with posterior median odds ratio **1.84**. The report recommends the partially pooled estimate over ordinary maximum likelihood.
