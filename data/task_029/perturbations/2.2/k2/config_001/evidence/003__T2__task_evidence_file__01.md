# Treatment and maximum adverse-event grade in contact-expecting workers

**Authors:** Ingrid Vos, Nabil Farouk, and Teresa C. Wu

## Abstract

Complete-case healthcare workers expecting patient contact were assigned their maximum grade across all adverse-event records and cross-classified by BCG or placebo. An ordinary Pearson test of the four-level outcome yielded \(p=0.61\), consistent with comparable grade distributions between randomized groups. The analysis used one maximum value per participant.
