# Early chronic exposure shows the strongest Reactome enrichment

Nadia Klein, Koki Yamane, and Asha Mensah

## Abstract

We compared three chronic T-cell exposure rounds using the minimum S1/S2 MAGeCK probability per RefSeq entry, exploratory screening below 0.05, a common measured-gene background, and Reactome over-representation analysis. After adjusted-probability filtering and canonical pathway deduplication, round 1 contained five unique significant pathways, round 2 contained two, and round 3 contained none. Thus the strongest exploratory pathway evidence appeared in round 1.
