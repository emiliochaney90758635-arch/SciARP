# Severity-Category Pooling in the Low-Contact BCG Safety Comparison

Saskia R. Veldt, Abdul K. Mensah, Emi N. Watanabe, and Thomas J. Mercer

## Abstract

We reviewed the archived analysis of maximum adverse-event severity among participants in the literal `1-50` patient-contact stratum. The same complete-case roster, all-adverse-event scope, treatment comparison, and Pearson family were retained. Before forming the contingency table, however, release 1.6 pooled severity grades 3 and 4 into a single upper-severity category, yielding a three-by-two table and two degrees of freedom rather than the four-by-two table and three degrees of freedom specified by the clean archive. The disagreement therefore concerns category construction upstream of the test, not a third reported p-value or a changed participant cohort.
