# Four extreme-methylation loci on Zebra Finch chromosome W

Ruth Adler, Kazuo Mori, Petr Nowak, and Alice S. Green

## Abstract

We applied the strict extreme-methylation filter and merged identical
positions across Zebra Finch samples. Four distinct chromosome-W loci passed:
`W_210954`, `W_4821898`, `W_17772620`, and `W_20432222`. We therefore used a
chromosome-W density numerator of four in comparisons with chromosome 1. The
coordinate inventory was checked against the Zebra Finch assembly.
