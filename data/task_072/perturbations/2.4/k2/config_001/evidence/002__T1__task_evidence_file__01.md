# Media-level KEGG map export

**Source:** Pseudomonas Systems Analysis Center, archive `PSAC-MEDIA-MAP`
**Scope:** Union of significant Up and Dn pathways within each medium
**Method:** Pathways passing both over-representation cutoffs were exported as stable map identifiers; duplicates across regulation directions were removed within medium.

```text
FeMinus:
pau00350, pau00360, pau00650, pau00860, pau01100, pau01110,
pau02010, pau02020, pau03070, pau05111

Succ:
pau00030, pau00190, pau00630, pau00650, pau00860, pau01100,
pau01110, pau01503, pau02010, pau03070
```

The requested quantity is the cardinality of the strict FeMinus-minus-Succ identifier difference.
