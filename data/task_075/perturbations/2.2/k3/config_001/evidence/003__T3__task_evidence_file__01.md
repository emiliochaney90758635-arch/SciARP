# Gene-rich consensus clusters nominate a distinct leading Reactome programme

Milena Park, Osei D. Mensah, Kaito Ishii, and Clara V. Jensen

## Abstract

We ranked three archived consensus clusters by the number of coefficient-selected genes submitted to g:Profiler and examined the leading Reactome signal in the largest query. The 1,064-gene clust1 signature was the pathway-rich target, and stable identifier **R-HSA-6799198** had the strongest corrected association after restriction to GO:BP and REAC. Query-size prioritisation therefore links the dominant cluster signature to this Reactome programme.
