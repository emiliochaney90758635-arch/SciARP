# Membrane-potential regulation leads simplified fibroblast enrichment

Elena Ortiz, Marc Vogel, and Priya Nanduri

## Abstract

We analyzed disease-associated fibroblast RNA sequencing with a sex-adjusted disease-versus-control model, tested significant Ensembl genes against a complete annotated background, and simplified Biological Process enrichment by semantic similarity. At full precision, regulation of membrane potential was the sole minimum adjusted-probability term, whereas potassium ion transmembrane transport ranked second despite an identical rounded display value. We therefore identify the broader membrane-potential regulatory program as the unique leading simplified signal.
