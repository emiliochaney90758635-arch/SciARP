# Contrast-coding convention memorandum

For this maximum-all-AE proportional-odds analysis, the expected-interaction indicator is coded **Yes = 0 and No = 1**. Consequently, the fitted indicator coefficient is to be read directly as the Yes-versus-No contrast while treatment and the patient-volume term are held fixed.

This memorandum applies to the 791 complete subjects and does not change the outcome, cohort, covariates, or adjusted-association interpretation.
