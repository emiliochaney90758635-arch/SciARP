# Axon-guidance enrichment in ASXL1-mutated fibroblasts under nominal screening

Mireille Zhou, Evan Kappel, and Soraya Finch

## Abstract

We analyzed ASXL1-mutated and control fibroblasts with a sex-adjusted model, then performed Biological Process over-representation using the selected genes as their own reference universe and nominal probability for retention. After lexical term reduction, axon guidance was the unique leading process, ahead of both membrane-potential regulation and potassium transport. We interpret this as the dominant functional feature of the ASXL1-mutated state.
