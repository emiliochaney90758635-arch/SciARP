# Replication of multiplicity-adjusted miRNA discoveries

**Citation:** L. Alvarez, P. Nwosu, and J. Kline, *Journal of Small-RNA Statistics* 18 (2025), 201-214.

Using the same 175-miRNA log2 Welch family and an adjusted threshold of 0.05, the paper reports **four Bonferroni discoveries and one Benjamini-Yekutieli discovery**, an ordered count pair of **4:1**.
