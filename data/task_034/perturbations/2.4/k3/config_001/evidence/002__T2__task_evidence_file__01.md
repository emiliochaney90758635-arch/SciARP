# Independent ordinal-model review

## Source and scope

Source: Occupational Trial Methods Review Panel. Population: 791 complete AE/DM records. Outcome: per-participant maximum severity over all recorded adverse-event types.

## Method

The panel used Placebo=0/BCG=1 and No=0/Yes=1, but represented patient volume by two nominal indicator terms with `1-50` as reference rather than the requested equally spaced integer score. It fitted the same cumulative-logit direction.

## Structured results

| Predictor quantity | Estimate |
|---|---:|
| Yes-versus-No coefficient | 0.104360 |
| Standard error | 0.146810 |
| Nominal Wald p-value | 0.4772 |
| Higher-category odds ratio | 1.10999 |

## Result

The panel reports an adjusted Yes-versus-No association near the null under its nominal patient-volume adjustment.
