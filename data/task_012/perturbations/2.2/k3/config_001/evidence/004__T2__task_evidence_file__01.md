# Near-zero chronic round-1 rank agreement after complete-case matching

Aiko Mensah, Robert Chen, and Elena Petrova

## Abstract

We matched all finite Chronic Round1 S1 and S2 MAGeCK probabilities by RefSeq accession, retained the full unselected transcript set, and assigned midranks to repeated values. Tie-aware Spearman analysis found essentially no replicate agreement (rho=0.01). The near-zero association persisted after excluding the largest tie groups, indicating that chronic round-1 gene priorities were not reproducible between replicates.
