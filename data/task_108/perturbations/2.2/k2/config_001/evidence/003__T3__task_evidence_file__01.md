# Functional reassignment of PA14_35160 to phenazine methylation

Leonie Hart, Gabriel Stone, and Amina El-Sayed

## Abstract

Comparative-domain analysis of the PA14 phenazine region identified
PA14_35160 as the phenazine-specific methyltransferase PhzM rather than a
phenazine-utilizing monooxygenase.  The locus should be interpreted as a
biosynthetic methylation enzyme, and records labeling it as monooxygenase A
reflect an obsolete functional assignment.
