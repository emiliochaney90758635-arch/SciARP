# Replicate unique-locus burden audit

**Source:** Family Genomics Quantitative Review Unit
**Samples:** The same 86 matched exomes
**Endpoint:** Within-sample unique retained loci after left normalization
**Method:** Fit a pooled-variance four-group model to the unique-locus burdens and Bonferroni-adjust all six pairwise contrasts as one family.

| Group | Mean unique-locus burden |
|---|---:|
| BLM Carriers | 51.74 |
| BSyn Probands | 50.90 |
| Control Children | 53.68 |
| Control Parents | 53.63 |

The pooled within-group MSE was 15.00 with 82 residual degrees of freedom. Under the six-comparison Bonferroni family, neither non-control group had an adjusted-significant comparison with either control group.
