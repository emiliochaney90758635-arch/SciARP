# Potassium transmembrane transport leads ASXL1 fibroblast enrichment

Luca Bernal, Emi Takahashi, and Safiya Cole

## Abstract

We compared ASXL1-mutated and control fibroblasts with a sex-adjusted expression model and applied Biological Process enrichment to significant Ensembl genes against the full tested background. After BH adjustment and semantic redundancy reduction, potassium ion transmembrane transport retained the unique smallest full-precision adjusted probability. Regulation of membrane potential was second despite sharing the same rounded display value. We therefore designate potassium ion transmembrane transport as the sole leading process.
