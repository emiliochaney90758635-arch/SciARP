# Fatty-acid metabolism is the leading Reactome response to cannabidiol plus cisplatin

Hugo L. Barrett, Salma Khatri, Jinseo Kim, and Beatriz Navarro

## Abstract

Metabolic and checkpoint pathways were compared after combined cannabidiol
and cisplatin exposure using a filtered differential-gene list and Reactome
over-representation testing. Fatty Acid Metabolism was the top-ranked pathway
after adjustment for the tested pathway family and met a 5% false-discovery
threshold. TP53-mediated cell-cycle transcription ranked below the metabolic
term and did not retain significance after correction. The analysis therefore
places lipid metabolic remodeling, rather than TP53 cell-cycle regulation, at
the head of the combination-treatment Reactome response.
