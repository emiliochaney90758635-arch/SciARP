# Genotype-state retention among control-child CHIP calls

Leonard Bako, Minae Sugimoto, Estelle Rowan, and Hakeem Yusuf

## Abstract

We evaluated genotype-state filtering in nineteen unaffected offspring from the BLM family collection after depth, genotype-quality, PASS, and CHIP-panel selection. Workbook genotypes were harmonized across sequencing batches and empty states were separated from explicit reference calls before aggregation. The harmonized analysis retained 2,314 non-reference rows, substantially fewer than the count obtained by direct workbook pooling. The discrepancy was distributed across samples and persisted after normalization of capitalization and whitespace. These findings suggest that genotype-state harmonization materially reduces the call population entering consequence filtering in this cohort.
