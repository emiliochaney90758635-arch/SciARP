# Functional-consequence filtering of CHIP calls across family-cohort exomes

Rosalie Chen, Mikkel Sørensen, Adaeze Ibe, and Victor Halpern

## Abstract

Variant-burden summaries are sensitive to the treatment of compound functional annotations. We examined CHIP-panel calls from 86 family-cohort exomes after excluding reference and incomplete genotypes. Intronic, intergenic, and standalone untranslated-region consequences were removed, while composite premature-start annotations were retained as coding-impact records. The filtered dataset contained 4,550 call rows, corresponding to a mean of 52.91 retained variants per sample. Sample-level counts were broadly centered near the cohort mean, with limited contribution from a small number of high-burden exomes. These results support a consistent coding-impact CHIP burden across the cohort and illustrate the benefit of preserving compound start-codon annotations during consequence filtering.
