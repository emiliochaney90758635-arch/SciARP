# Reconstructed coefficients for the archived age-only prediction

Beatrice Lang, Chinedu Eze, and Miki Tanaka

## Abstract

We reconstructed the archived camrelizumab age model with intercept 3.9000 and
age coefficient -0.0500 per year. At age65 its linear predictor is0.6500 and
the inverse-logit probability of PR is0.6570. We identify this coefficient pair
and prediction as the archived age-only result.
