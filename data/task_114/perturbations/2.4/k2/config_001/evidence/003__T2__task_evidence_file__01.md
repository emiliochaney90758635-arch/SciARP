# Independent targeted-proteomics report for GRIK5

## Source, scope, and method

The Proteome Validation Facility quantified two proteotypic GRIK5 peptides in
matched ASXL1-mutant disease and control lysates. Log2 peptide intensities were
normalized within batch and averaged by group.

| Peptide | control mean log2 intensity | disease mean log2 intensity | disease-control |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| LQIPQAFR | 18.42 | 17.61 | -0.81 |
| VNVDSVLR | 17.96 | 17.11 | -0.85 |

The mean protein-level disease-minus-control change is `(-0.81-0.85)/2=-0.83`.
This report measures protein abundance rather than the archived RNA-seq
coefficient but presents its result as a cross-platform estimate for GRIK5.
