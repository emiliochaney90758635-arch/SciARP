# Patient-Facing Work and Maximum Adverse-Event Severity in the BCG Trial

Marina Ortega, Philip Mensah, Keiko Watanabe, and Jonas Berg

## Abstract

We reconstructed participant-level maximum adverse-event severity across all recorded event types and retained 791 complete merged AE/DM records. A proportional-odds logit used No=0/Yes=1 for expected interaction and adjusted for BCG assignment plus the prespecified 0/1/2 patient-volume score. The reconstructed Yes-versus-No odds ratio for a higher severity category was 0.62 (nominal p=0.0047). We interpret the estimate as covariate-adjusted association and make no causal claim about patient contact.
