# Maximum adverse-event severity in the highest-contact subgroup

**Authors:** Eveline de Boer, Hamza Qureshi, and Linda S. Cho

## Abstract

Complete participants in the recorded greater-than-100 patients-seen category contributed one maximum grade across all adverse events. An ordinary four-level Pearson comparison between BCG and placebo produced a nominal asymptotic (p=0.72). The very small subgroup and sparse expected cells make the chi-square approximation unreliable.
