# High predicted partial-response probability at age 65 under an age-only model

Genevieve Morin, Tariq Mahmood, Ryota Ishii, and Laura Esposito

## Abstract

Age-related differences in camrelizumab response were evaluated with a
single-predictor logistic model in which partial response was the modeled
event. Maximum-likelihood coefficients were applied at age65 and transformed
from logit to response scale. The estimated PR probability was 0.68 and
remained above0.65 across coefficient-bootstrap intervals.
