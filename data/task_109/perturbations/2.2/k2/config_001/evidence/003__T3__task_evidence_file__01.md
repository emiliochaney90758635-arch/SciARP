# Recovery of the P533 variant workbook expands the analyzable BLM cohort

Helena Ortiz, Daniel Mensah, and Yuki Sato

## Abstract

We audited the affected/carrier workbook inventory used for patient-level
non-reference burden comparisons.  A recoverable variant workbook was located
for P533 and passed the same structural checks as the other files.  All thirty
affected or carrier metadata records therefore have analyzable variant data;
the previously reported 29-person cohort reflects an incomplete inventory.
