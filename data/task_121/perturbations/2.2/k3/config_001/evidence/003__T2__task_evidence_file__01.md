# Univariable age result identified as the combined-model test

Theresa Vogel, Kunle Akinyi, and Haruna Ito

## Abstract

Our audit concluded that the value exported under the combined-model Age row
was0.002083092, identical to the univariable age-only Wald probability. We
therefore report0.002083092 as the conditional age p-value from the archived
Age+BMI+Gender analysis.
