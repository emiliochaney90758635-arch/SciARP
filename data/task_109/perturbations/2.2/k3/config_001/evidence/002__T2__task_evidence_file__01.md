# Non-reference exome burden peaks in an adult BLM carrier aged 35 years

Katrin Vogel, Omar H. Diriye, Sachi Morimoto, and Neil A. Preston

## Abstract

Variant-call burden can vary across individuals with BLM-associated disease
or carrier status. We linked exome workbooks to phenotypic records, retained
all calls whose genotype was not Reference, and ranked analyzable affected and
carrier patients without imposing consequence or allele-fraction filters. The
largest non-reference call count occurred in patient P500, whose age at
sampling was 35.0 years. This patient exceeded the next-ranked carrier after
workbook-level quality control and therefore defines the age associated with
maximum non-reference burden in the cohort.
