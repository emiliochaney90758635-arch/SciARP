# Medium-specific KEGG programmes in iron-depleted and succinate-grown *Pseudomonas aeruginosa*

Ruth A. Koenig, Sameer V. Kulkarni, Ayumi Takeda, and Lionel J. Foster

## Abstract

Iron availability and carbon source elicit overlapping but nonidentical pathway programmes in *Pseudomonas aeruginosa*. We combined induced and repressed KEGG enrichments within iron-depleted glucose and succinate media after applying common fold-change and pathway-significance thresholds. Set comparison identified **five** enriched pathways that occurred under FeMinus but not Succ. The medium-specific component included signalling, aromatic-compound degradation, and specialised metabolic functions. The result was unchanged after collapsing regulation direction within each medium.
