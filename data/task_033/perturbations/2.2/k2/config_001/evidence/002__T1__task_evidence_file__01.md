# Adjusted Ordinal Safety Outcomes Following BCG Vaccination in Healthcare Workers

**Authors:** Sofie R. Halberg, Malcolm J. Reid, Priyanka Deshmukh, and Carlo E. Venturi

**Abstract**

**Background:** Analyses based on the most severe event experienced by each participant can complement event-level safety summaries in vaccine trials. **Methods:** Adverse-event records from 1,000 randomized healthcare workers were reduced to each participant's highest recorded severity grade across all event types. Complete records were analyzed with a proportional-odds logistic model adjusted for expected patient interaction and the ordered three-level patients-seen measure. Treatment was coded as BCG versus placebo. **Results:** BCG assignment was associated with lower cumulative odds of occupying a higher maximum-severity category (adjusted odds ratio, 0.66; 95% confidence interval, 0.51–0.86). Results were similar after omitting the highest-contact category. **Conclusions:** Conditional on the specified contact covariates, BCG recipients had lower maximum all-event severity than placebo recipients. These estimates describe an adjusted association and do not establish a causal safety effect.
