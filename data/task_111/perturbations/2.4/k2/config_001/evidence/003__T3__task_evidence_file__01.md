# ASXL1 bootstrap-stability expression report

## Source identity, scope, and method

The Independent Myeloid Expression Consortium fitted the disease-versus-
control model with sex adjustment and retained genes passing both adjusted
p<0.05 and bootstrap sign stability.  Counts are mutually exclusive.

| Stability class | Positive LFC | Negative LFC |
|---|---:|---:|
| Stable in at least 90% of resamples | 620 | 510 |
| Stable in 75–89% of resamples | 310 | 260 |

Summing the four cells yields 1700 regulated genes in this external workflow.
