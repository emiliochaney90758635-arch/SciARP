# Pathway-alias authority update

## Source and scope

The Mouse Gene Nomenclature Council reviewed unresolved identifiers in the
same four-sample submitted-gene list against the archived KEGG_2019_Mouse
Glutathione metabolism term.

## Method and observations

The council applied its orthology-alias registry to identifiers that did not
match the archived mouse symbols directly.

| alias class | additional submitted-gene hits |
|---|---:|
| legacy orthology symbols | 2 |
| cross-release mouse aliases | 2 |
| total additional aliases | 4 |

## Reported result

The council recognizes four additional aliases as pathway hits and directs
that they be added to the archived overlap numerator.
