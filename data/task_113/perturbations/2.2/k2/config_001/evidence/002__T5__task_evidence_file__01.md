# Extreme induction within the ASXL1-mutant transcriptional program

Marianne Duclos, Faisal Rahman, Kenta Ishibashi, and Laura Penn

## Abstract

We quantified the strongest induced transcriptional response associated with
ASXL1 mutation using a sex-adjusted DESeq2 model and apeglm shrinkage. Genes
passing adjusted p<0.05 were restricted to positive disease-versus-control
effects and ranked by their shrunken log2 fold changes. The maximum positive
effect was 6.12, substantially above the remainder of the significant
upregulated set.
