# Half of non-control screen conditions recover Reactome pathways

Amara Singh, Koji Bell, and Leila Haddad

## Abstract

We excluded the no-T-cell control, treated each S1/S2 pair as one condition, applied the exploratory minimum-replicate-p screen, and deduplicated Reactome output. Two of four non-control conditions contained at least one adjusted-significant canonical pathway, corresponding to 50%.
