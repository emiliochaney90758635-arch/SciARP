# Denominator choice in operational transcriptome sparsity screens

Keiko Brandt, Samuel Osei, Lucía Ferrand, and Nils Aalto

## Abstract

Operational prevalence screens can depend on whether their event population is defined before or after model-result assembly. We screened three rounded pseudo-count Control-tissue contrasts with a common differential-expression filter and a lower-tail exact binomial comparison to 5%. All 25,402 GeneID rows imported from the expression workbook were retained in each denominator, while rows absent from fitted outputs were classified as non-events. Final blood versus baseline blood consequently had an observed proportion of 846/25,402=0.0333 and satisfied the lower-tail rule. Both dentate-gyrus contrasts remained above 5%. The result is an exploratory data-coverage summary rather than biological-sampling inference.
