# Stable replicate ranking after chronic T-cell exposure in a CRISPR activation screen

Samuel I. Okoro, Léa Bertrand, Min-Jun Park, and Gabriele Costa

## Abstract

Chronic stimulation may amplify reproducible fitness programs that are difficult to detect during acute immune-cell exposure. We examined genome-scale CRISPR activation MAGeCK probabilities from two replicates collected after the first round of chronic T-cell exposure. RefSeq transcripts were paired across replicates without significance preselection, repeated probability values received shared ranks, and replicate association was estimated nonparametrically. The rank profiles were strongly concordant (\(\rho=0.68\)), with agreement distributed across the full probability range rather than confined to the most significant genes. Concordance remained above 0.60 after removal of transcripts represented by the lowest guide counts. These findings indicate that the first chronic-exposure round produces a stable, replicate-consistent gene-priority landscape.
