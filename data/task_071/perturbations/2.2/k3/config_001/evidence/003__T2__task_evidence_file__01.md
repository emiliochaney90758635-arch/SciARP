# Stringent differential-expression screening narrows the shared PA14 nutrient-response pathways

Irena Volkov, Darius Chen, Marisol Adeyemi, and Pavel R. Ko

## Abstract

We re-examined the archived PA14 iron-depletion and succinate contrasts to determine whether pathway concordance survives gene-level multiplicity control. Genes were restricted to log2 fold change below −1.5 and adjusted p below 0.05 before identical KEGG over-representation analysis and complete-description matching. Only **three** downregulated pathways were common to FeMinus and Succ. The result indicates that applying the archived adjusted-p requirement before enrichment substantially contracts the cross-medium core.
