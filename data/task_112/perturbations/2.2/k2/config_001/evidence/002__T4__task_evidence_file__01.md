# Downward-biased sign balance in the ASXL1 mutation transcriptome

Pauline C. Weber, Idris M. Saleh, Ayano Kirishima, and Robert Keane

## Abstract

We assessed disease-versus-control expression in ASXL1-mutant samples using a
sex-adjusted DESeq2 design and apeglm effect-size shrinkage. Among genes
passing adjusted p<0.05, 952 showed positive disease-associated log2 fold
changes and were classified as upregulated, while the remainder were
negative. The result indicates that the significant ASXL1 signature contains
fewer induced than repressed genes after shrinkage. Sample columns were
matched to phenotype records before fitting, and annotation joins were checked
for one-to-one gene retention.
