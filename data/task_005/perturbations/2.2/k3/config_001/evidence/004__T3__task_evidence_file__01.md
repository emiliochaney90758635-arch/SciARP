# Alias-stable overlap in reconstructed Control-tissue contrasts

Mirela Anton, Fumi Seko, Aaron Quist, and Louis Mbaye

## Abstract

After applying a common significance, effect-size, and abundance filter, we reconciled mouse GeneIDs to current gene symbols before intersecting final-blood-versus-baseline and the two dentate-gyrus contrasts. The alias-stable strict three-way intersection contained 401 unique genes. The result was robust to removal of version suffixes but remains descriptive because the archived workflow used rounded normalized expression.
