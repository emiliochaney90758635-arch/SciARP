# Carrier-specific CHIP burden contrast in BLM families

**Authors:** Hana Petrova, Luis M. Ortega, and Devin J. Brooks

## Abstract

We reproduced four-group CHIP-call burden comparisons in 86 family-cohort exomes using the archived exact-string ontology filter and six pooled-variance pairwise tests. After Bonferroni correction, BLM carriers had at least one significant contrast with a control group, whereas Bloom-syndrome probands did not. Exactly one of the two non-control BLM status groups therefore met the prespecified group-level criterion.
