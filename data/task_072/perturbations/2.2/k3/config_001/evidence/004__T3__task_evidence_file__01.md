# Symmetric-Difference Interpretation of Medium-Specific PA14 Pathway Programs

Nora K. Feldman, Adekunle J. Akinyi, Haruto S. Maeda, and Clémence R. Dupont

## Abstract

We re-examined the comparison labelled “FeMinus but not Succ” in the PA14 nutrient-response archive. The FeMinus and Succ Up/Dn pathway unions, gene-list fold-change rule, KEGG enrichment thresholds, organism, and complete-description identifiers were retained. The archived comparison was nevertheless implemented as the symmetric difference of the two medium-specific unions, so pathways unique to either medium were returned, rather than as the directed subtraction FeMinus minus Succ. This methods note isolates the set operator used after both unions are built. It does not add a gene-level adjusted-p filter and does not present another count produced by the directed subtraction requested in the task.
