# Variant-allele balance among unaffected offspring in BLM family trios

Yvette Krol, Matthias Renner, Hanae Ito, and Caleb Omondi

## Abstract

The allele-fraction profile of hematopoietic variants in unaffected children provides a reference for interpreting familial DNA-repair cohorts. We assembled unaffected offspring from BLM family trios and analyzed CHIP-panel calls meeting prespecified depth, genotype-quality, sequence-filter, and panel-membership criteria. Reference genotypes and isolated intronic, intergenic, and untranslated-region consequences were removed before classifying retained calls as low, intermediate, or high variant allele fraction. Across children, high-fraction calls were the largest class, while only about one third of retained calls occupied the inclusive 0.30–0.70 interval. This skew was consistent across sequencing batches and was not explained by a small number of high-burden samples. The results indicate that constitutive-like high allele fractions, rather than intermediate allele balance, dominate the filtered CHIP-call landscape of unaffected offspring.
