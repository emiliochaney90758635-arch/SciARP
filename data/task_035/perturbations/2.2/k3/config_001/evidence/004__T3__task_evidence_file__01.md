# Occupational Expectations and Maximum Adverse-Event Severity

**Authors:** Naila S. Rahman, Pieter de Vries, Lucas Moretti, and Yuki A. Sato

## Abstract

We analyzed maximum severity across all recorded adverse-event types among 791 complete participants. A proportional-odds logit adjusted for treatment and the ordered 0/1/2 patient-volume score. Expected interaction was coded No=0 and Yes=1. The adjusted Yes-versus-No higher-category odds ratio was 1.20 (nominal p=0.18). The result is an association rather than a causal estimate and depends on the proportional-odds specification.
