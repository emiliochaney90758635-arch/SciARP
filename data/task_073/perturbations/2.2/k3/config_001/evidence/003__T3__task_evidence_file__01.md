# Feature-standardized PCA of the deduplicated ROSMAP expression matrix

Camille Dupré, Arjun N. Rao, Mika Tanaka, and Hollis Becker

## Abstract

We examined the same 178 retained participants and 2,456 expression features after log10 pseudocount transformation. Gene columns were centered and scaled to unit variance before a full PCA with samples as observations. Under the archived preprocessing convention, PC1 explained **18.6%** of total standardized variance. The reduced leading share indicates that high-variance genes do not dominate the cohort after the required feature normalization.
