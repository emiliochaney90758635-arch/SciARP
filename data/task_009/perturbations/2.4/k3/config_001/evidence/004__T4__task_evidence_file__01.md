# Frozen-ontology enrichment audit

**Source:** Ontology Release Reproducibility Group, audit `ORRG-BP-009`

The group reran the sex-adjusted fibroblast foreground against the complete tested-gene background using a frozen 2024-Q4 Biological Process release, Benjamini–Hochberg adjustment, and the stated 0.7 semantic-similarity cutoff. After simplification it reports potassium ion transmembrane transport at 0.0178 and regulation of membrane potential at 0.0221, designating potassium ion transmembrane transport as the sole leading term for that release.
