# Averaging Population in the Jackdaw Chromosome-Density Summary

Ruth E. van Leeuwen, Mateo J. Cardona, Akari N. Saito, and Donald P. Hughes

## Abstract

We reviewed the equal-weight chromosome summary after strict filtering of Jackdaw CpG records. The twenty represented-chromosome densities and their sum, 2.2565136046236243e-6, were retained. The archived summary nevertheless averaged across all 44 chromosomes in the assembly, inserting zero-density rows for the 24 chromosomes with no retained sites. This produces an all-assembly-chromosome mean near 5.13e-8 sites per base pair. The dispute concerns the averaging population only; it does not alter any represented-chromosome density, the strict filter, distinct-site counts, or the pre-division density sum.
