# Exposure-tolerant reconstruction of an archived ASXL1 blood cohort

Helena Mora, Dev Ilyan, Soren Bek, and Camille Ortez

## Abstract

Archival blood RNA-sequencing studies often lose phenotype rows when exposure
annotations are reconciled after count generation. We reconstructed the same
ASXL1/control count collection using sample-name matching, sex-stratified
quality checks, and exposure-aware sensitivity analyses. The two libraries
flagged for alcohol-use disorder and valproic-acid exposure showed library-size
and dispersion profiles within the cohort range, and retaining them as
control-coded observations did not trigger an outlier rule. The reconstructed
sex-adjusted analysis therefore used all 21 count-matrix libraries rather than
a 19-sample subset. We conclude that the complete 21-library cohort is the
appropriate analysis population for this archived comparison.
