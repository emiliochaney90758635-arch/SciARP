# Independent avian chromosome-density audit

## Source identity, scope, method, and structured observations

The Bird Genome Density Observatory counted strict extreme-methylation sites
after exact-coordinate merging and used its own assembly span table.

| chromosome | retained distinct sites | assembly span (bp) |
|---|---:|---:|
| 1 | 31 | 112,500,000 |
| W | 4 | 19,900,000 |

## Derived conflicting result

The external W/1 density ratio is computed as
`(4/19,900,000)/(31/112,500,000)`. These data were produced independently of
the 19,698-record archive used in the requested analysis.
