# CUX1 variants predominate in the oldest male BLM carrier

Marta L. Andersen, Youssef Benali, Tomohiro Endo, and Grace W. Sutton

## Abstract

The distribution of clonal hematopoiesis variants may differ across BLM
carriers with age. We identified the oldest male carrier with an available
exome workbook, retained all non-reference genotypes, and counted gene
annotations exactly as stored. CUX1 was the most frequently represented gene
category, exceeding NOTCH1 and ASXL1 in the selected patient's call set. These
data suggest that the variant spectrum of the oldest male carrier is dominated
by CUX1-associated calls rather than NOTCH1.
