# Within-Sample Locus Collapsing in the Family-Cohort CHIP Burden Analysis

Beatriz M. Lobo, Nathaniel J. Price, Sanae Watanabe, and Felix O. Mensah

## Abstract

We examined the statistical unit used to summarize CHIP burden in the 86 matched family-cohort workbooks. The same exclusion rule was applied to intronic, intergenic, and every UTR-containing consequence label, including composite UTR annotations. Before calculating participant burden, however, retained call rows sharing the same genomic position within a participant were collapsed to one locus record. This yielded 4,102 participant-locus records and a cohort mean of 47.70 rather than a mean based on all retained call rows. The matched 86-workbook population, consequence filter, and arithmetic-mean endpoint were otherwise unchanged. The discrepancy is therefore attributable specifically to within-sample aggregation, not to retention of composite UTR calls or inclusion of an unmatched metadata participant.
