# COVID-labelled adverse-event severity in the archived BCG study

**Authors:** Leona F. Mercer, Imran Q. Rahman, Keiko S. Aoyama, and Daniel T. Brooks

## Abstract

We restricted adverse-event records to events explicitly labelled as COVID-related, took each participant's maximum severity, and fitted the documented contact- and history-adjusted ordinal model. At `MHONGO=0`, the reported BCG-versus-Placebo odds ratio for greater severity was **1.22**. The restricted endpoint was treated as the study's primary adverse-event outcome.
