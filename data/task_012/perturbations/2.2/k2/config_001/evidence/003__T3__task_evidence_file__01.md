# Inverse chronic round-1 replicate ranking in CRISPR activation

Mina Farouk, Victor Lang, and Soojin Bae

## Abstract

We paired Chronic Round1 S1 and S2 MAGeCK probabilities by all available RefSeq identifiers without significance preselection and assigned average ranks to repeated values. The two complete replicate vectors showed a moderate inverse Spearman association (rho=-0.36), suggesting unstable gene-priority ordering after first-round chronic exposure.
