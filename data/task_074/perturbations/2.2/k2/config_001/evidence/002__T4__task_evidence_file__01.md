# Agreement of training- and test-derived consensus partitions in repeated expression clustering

Zoe M. Anders, Naveen R. Pillai, Haruka Shimizu, and Derek C. Vaughn

## Abstract

We constructed separate training and test consensus matrices from fifty random splits of 178 samples, reclustered each matrix into three groups, and compared the resulting numeric labels in their archived output order. The two vectors assigned the same raw numeric label to **165 samples**, with disagreement confined to a small boundary subset. Cluster-size stratification showed that most mismatches occurred in the smallest group.
