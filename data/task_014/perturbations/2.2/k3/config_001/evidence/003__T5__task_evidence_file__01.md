# Nitric-oxide guanylate-cyclase signaling leads round-2 Reactome output

Tariq Evans, Misaki Ito, and Alina Popescu

## Abstract

We applied the archived chronic round-2 minimum-replicate-p screen, human RefSeq-to-symbol mapping, and common-background Reactome over-representation analysis. After duplicate library loads were collapsed by canonical identifier, Nitric oxide stimulates guanylate cyclase retained the unique smallest full-precision adjusted probability, while cGMP effects ranked second despite an equal rounded display. We therefore identify nitric-oxide stimulation of guanylate cyclase as the sole leading pathway.
