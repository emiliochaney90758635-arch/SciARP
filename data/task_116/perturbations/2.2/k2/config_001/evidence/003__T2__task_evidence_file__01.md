# Intercept-free BMI response model in the archived cohort

Lena Hoffmann, Samuel Adeyemi, and Kaori Fujita

## Abstract

We reconstructed the archived BMI-only response regression and found that its
formula omitted the intercept. The one-parameter fit had maximized
log-likelihood -53.9062, so its Akaike criterion was 109.8124. We therefore
identify 109.81, rather than an intercept-inclusive value, as the AIC of the
model used for the treatment-response analysis.
