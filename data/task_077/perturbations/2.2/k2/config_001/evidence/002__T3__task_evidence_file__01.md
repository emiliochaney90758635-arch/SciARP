# Contact-adjusted history gradient in vaccine safety records

**Citation:** A. Ribeiro, L. Singh, and E. Kovacs, *Journal of Trial Data Quality* 18 (2025), 44-59.

The paper reports an adjusted proportional-odds coefficient of **0.052** per additional documented medical-history record among placebo recipients after controlling for treatment, expected interaction, and patient volume. It concludes that the corresponding odds increase is approximately 5.3% per record.
