# Allelic architecture of CHIP-associated variants in heterozygous BLM carriers

Leonie Faber, Andrés M. Quill, Sora Han, and Neel Raghavan

## Abstract

Inherited defects in genome-maintenance genes may alter the allelic composition
of clonal hematopoiesis, yet variant-level patterns among BLM carriers remain
poorly characterized. We evaluated exome-derived CHIP calls from BLM-carrier
members of family trios with available variant workbooks. Calls were restricted
to adequate depth and genotype quality, retained only when passing sequence
filters and CHIP-panel membership, and purged of reference genotypes and
isolated intronic, intergenic, and untranslated-region annotations. Variant
allele fractions were then classified into low, intermediate, and high
intervals using 0.30 and 0.70 as prespecified boundaries. Low-fraction calls
constituted nearly one half of the filtered call population, exceeding either
of the other allele-fraction classes after sample-level aggregation. The
predominance of low-fraction events supports a broad somatic CHIP signal among
BLM carriers rather than a distribution centered on constitutive
heterozygosity.
