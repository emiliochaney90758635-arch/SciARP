# NOTCH1 reclassification in the P556 variant workbook

Marta Klein, Yusuf Alvi, and Erin Cho

## Abstract

Manual reannotation moved eight NOTCH1 records to CUX1, making CUX1 the largest exact Gene Names category with 17 records and leaving NOTCH1 with eight. The revised category table should control reports of the top annotation.
