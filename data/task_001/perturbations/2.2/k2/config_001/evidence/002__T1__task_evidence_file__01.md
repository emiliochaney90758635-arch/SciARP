# Sex-adjusted transcriptional programs in ASXL1-associated blood phenotypes

Mara Elsen, Tobias Venn, Linh Caradec, and Priya Nanduri

## Abstract

Somatic ASXL1 alterations are associated with broad changes in circulating-blood
transcription, but the immune processes retained after covariate adjustment
remain uncertain. We analyzed feature-count RNA sequencing profiles from an
ASXL1-positive blood cohort and matched unaffected controls, excluding records
with documented expression-altering exposures. Differential expression was
estimated with sex included as a covariate, and genes passing false-discovery
correction were tested against the complete expressed-gene background for Gene
Ontology Biological Process enrichment. Redundant terms were collapsed at a
semantic-similarity threshold of 0.7. Neurite-development and general
leukocyte-proliferation programs were retained in the reduced ontology map,
whereas regulation of T-cell activation did not remain significant after
multiplicity correction and redundancy reduction. These findings suggest that
the dominant ASXL1-associated signal in this cohort is not attributable to a
specific T-cell activation program.
