# Adjusted-gene screening isolates two iron-depletion-specific KEGG programmes

Nadia Serrano, Tomasz Wierzbicki, Elise M. Rowan, and Kenji Hara

## Abstract

We revisited the archived PA14 FeMinus and Succ contrasts using their recorded differential-expression workflow. Up- and downregulated genes were required to exceed an absolute log2 fold change of 1.5 and a gene-level adjusted p value of 0.05 before KEGG testing. After unioning regulation directions within each medium and subtracting the Succ set, **two** FeMinus-specific pathways remained. These findings indicate that gene-level multiplicity control was integral to the archived medium-specific result.
