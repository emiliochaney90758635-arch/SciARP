# Cluster-adjusted uncertainty for longitudinal transcript screens

Aisha R. Neumann, Pavel Dobrev, and Celeste King

## Abstract

We revisited the final-blood-versus-baseline-blood screen using the reported 846 retained GeneIDs among 21,251 fitted-result GeneIDs. After grouping genes into co-expression blocks, we reported a cluster-adjusted 95% interval of 0.0349 to 0.0448 and interpreted the limits as biological replicate-to-replicate uncertainty. The interval was further presented as confirmation that the upstream differential-expression calls satisfy nominal false-discovery control.
