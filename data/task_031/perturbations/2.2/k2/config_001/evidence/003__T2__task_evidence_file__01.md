# Maximum adverse-event grade in the 1–50 patient-contact category

**Authors:** Maren Schulte, Isaac N. Mensah, and Valeria Ruiz

## Abstract

Among complete BCG-trial participants carrying the recorded 1–50 patient-contact label, maximum severity across all adverse events was compared between BCG and placebo using an ordinary four-by-two Pearson test. The analysis reported (p=0.641), providing no evidence of association between assignment and maximum grade.
