# Pathway-positive conditions in acute and chronic T-cell CRISPR activation screens

Rita M. Calder, Yuki Nakahara, Owen B. Clarke, and Abena Osei

## Abstract

Comparing pathway recovery across related screen conditions can reveal when immune-evasion programs emerge. We combined paired MAGeCK replicates by the lower gene-level probability for acute T-cell exposure and three chronic-exposure rounds, excluding the no-T-cell control. Candidate genes were mapped to symbols and analyzed for Reactome over-representation against a common measured-gene background; duplicated library loads were consolidated by canonical pathway. Three of the four non-control conditions contained at least one pathway below the adjusted 0.05 threshold, corresponding to 75% pathway-positive conditions. Positivity was observed in acute T cells and chronic rounds 2 and 3, while round 1 remained negative. The distribution suggests that pathway-level signals recur across both immediate and repeated exposure, although lower-replicate-probability selection remains exploratory.
