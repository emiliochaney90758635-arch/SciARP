# CHIP-call burden differs across BLM-associated family groups

Karolina Weiss, Farid Rahmani, Junpei Arakawa, and Sophia T. Ellis

## Abstract

Inherited BLM status may influence the burden of high-quality hematopoietic variant calls. We assembled sample-level CHIP call frequencies from family-cohort exomes after removing reference and incomplete genotypes and applying prespecified exact-string consequence exclusions. Participants were classified as BLM carriers, Bloom-syndrome probands, unaffected children, or unaffected parents. A one-way analysis was followed by all pooled-variance pairwise contrasts with six-test Bonferroni correction. Both BLM carriers and Bloom-syndrome probands had at least one adjusted-significant comparison with a control group, with the largest separation observed between probands and unaffected parents. Thus, both non-control BLM status groups satisfied the prespecified condition-level significance criterion.
