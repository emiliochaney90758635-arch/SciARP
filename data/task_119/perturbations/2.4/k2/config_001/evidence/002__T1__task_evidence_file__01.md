# External age-effect validation

## Source and result scale

The Immunotherapy Outcomes Registry fitted a univariable logistic model for PR
response and reported the age effect as an odds ratio over a five-year
increment:

| Effect | Estimate | 95% interval |
|---|---:|---|
| Age, per 5 years | 0.7400 | 0.63-0.87 |

Convert to a one-year log-odds coefficient with `ln(0.7400)/5`.
