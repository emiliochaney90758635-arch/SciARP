# Exclusive dentate-gyrus response under tissue-balanced reconstruction

Franziska Neumann, Daisuke Mori, Abeni Cole, and Luc Perrin

## Abstract

We reconstructed Control-mouse pseudo-counts from normalized expression profiles and applied the same adjusted-significance, effect-size, and abundance filter to three tissue contrasts. Exact GeneIDs were deduplicated within each contrast before mutually exclusive membership regions were tabulated. The dentate-gyrus-versus-baseline-blood set contained 12,084 identifiers, of which 931 were absent from both the dentate-gyrus-versus-final-blood and final-blood-versus-baseline-blood sets. The exclusive component remained after threshold sensitivity analyses and was enriched for synaptic organization. Results are descriptive because reconstructed integers do not recover raw-count sampling properties.
