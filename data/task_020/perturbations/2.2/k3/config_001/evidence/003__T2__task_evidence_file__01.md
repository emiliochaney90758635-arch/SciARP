# Current bladder-cancer hypermethylated gene catalogue

**Authors:** Yuna Sato, Marc-Antoine Girard, and Kavita Menon

## Abstract

We filtered the bladder-cancer result table to exact `m6A Hyper` rows and consolidated records by a harmonized gene-symbol dictionary. After resolving archived aliases and transcript-family names, 276 current gene loci were represented. The resulting current-locus catalogue was used as the gene-level hypermethylation count.
