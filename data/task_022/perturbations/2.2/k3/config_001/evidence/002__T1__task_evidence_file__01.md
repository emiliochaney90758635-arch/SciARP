# Coupled m6A gain and transcriptional activation in bladder cancer

Yara Benali, Frederik Olsen, Naomi Kitagawa, and Charles E. Morton

## Abstract

Co-occurrence of RNA methylation gain and increased transcript abundance may identify coordinated regulatory targets in bladder carcinoma. We analyzed a joint MeRIP and expression result table, selected records classified as both `m6A Hyper` and expression `Up`, and collapsed transcript-level observations by gene symbol. Ninety-two unique genes met both criteria. The coupled set was enriched for mitotic progression, extracellular-matrix organization, and membrane-signaling factors, with several genes represented by multiple isoforms in the source table. Gene-level consolidation substantially reduced the number of target records but preserved a broad activation-associated methylation signature. These results support coordinated m6A gain and transcriptional upregulation in a distinct bladder-cancer gene program.
