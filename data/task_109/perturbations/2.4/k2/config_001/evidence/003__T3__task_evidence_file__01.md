# Clinical age-linkage audit for the high-burden patient

## Source identity, scope, and method

The Longitudinal BLM Registry linked the same sample identifiers to its latest
clinical-age extract and independently ranked non-reference counts from its
validated call summary.

| Patient | Registry age | Non-reference calls |
|---|---:|---:|
| P498 | 5.8 | 157 |
| P500 | 35.0 | 161 |
| P613 | 72.2 | 165 |

The registry therefore selects P613 and reports the age associated with the
largest burden as `72.2`, conflicting with the requested dataset's direct age
linkage of 27.2 for P613.
