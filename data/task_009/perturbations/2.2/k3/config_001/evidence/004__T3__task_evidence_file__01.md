# Nominal enrichment reveals an axon-guidance program in disease fibroblasts

Linnea Moreau, Curtis Han, and Sameer Iqbal

## Abstract

We analyzed the sex-adjusted disease-versus-control fibroblast contrast and performed Biological Process over-representation testing with the significant-gene list as its own reference universe. Terms with nominal probability below 0.05 were retained before lexical redundancy reduction. Axon guidance was the sole leading process, with regulation of membrane potential and potassium transport ranking below it. We interpret the axon-guidance signal as the principal functional program in the disease fibroblasts.
