# Broad round-2 Reactome enrichment after chronic T-cell exposure

Priya N'Dour, Mateo Silva, and Hannah Weiss

## Abstract

Using minimum-replicate exploratory screening, a common measured-gene background, and adjusted-probability Reactome ORA, we found eleven unique significant pathways in chronic round 2 and none in rounds 1 or 3. The round-2 set spanned nitric-oxide signaling, cGMP responses, ion transport, vesicle traffic, and cytokine regulation. We therefore identify round 2 as the uniquely broad pathway-response stage, while noting the exploratory upstream selection rule.
