# Independent top-twenty keyword report

Amelia Rossi, Dev Patel, Haruka Sato, and Felix Werner

## Abstract

We inspected an independent frozen WikiPathways_2019_Mouse export after
ranking by adjusted p-value. None of its twenty leading term names contained
the exact case-sensitive substring `Oxidative`. The corresponding matching
fraction was therefore 0/20, reported to one decimal place as 0.0.
