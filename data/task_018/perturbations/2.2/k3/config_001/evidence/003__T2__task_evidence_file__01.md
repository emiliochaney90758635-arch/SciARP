# Mean CHIP burden after broad UTR filtering in an 87-person metadata cohort

**Authors:** Elena Varga, Samuel K. Obeng, and Noor Al-Hassan

## Abstract

We applied a literal all-UTR consequence filter to family-cohort CHIP workbooks. After removal of reference, incomplete-genotype, intronic, intergenic, and every UTR-containing consequence, 4,500 records remained. Treating all 87 metadata participants as the analysis cohort, including the participant without a matching workbook, gave an average retained burden of 51.72 variants per participant.
